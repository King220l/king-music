# King Music branding policy

The source code is available under the MIT License. That license permits forks and changes.

The names **King Music**, **King220l**, the King Music icon, and official-release claims identify
builds published by King220l. Modified distributions must be clearly marked as unofficial and
must not claim that their binaries were published, reviewed, or signed by King220l.

Official binaries are listed in the digitally signed manifest in `official/`.
